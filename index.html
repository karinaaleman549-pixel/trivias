How to play

1. Write your team name. Choose 3–4 members and an avatar for each.

2. Practice first (3 items). Then the official trivia (10 items).

3. You have 40 seconds per item. Choose ONE answer.

4. Each correct answer = 1 point (10 items = 10 points). Speed only breaks ties.

5. Order and options are different for each team. Do NOT leave this page: you lose half a point.
